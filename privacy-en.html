Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This is a translation. In case of doubt, the Portuguese version prevails.

In short
  • We only keep what is needed for the app to work: the parents' email and what the family writes in the app.
  • Children do not create an account, do not give an email address and do not talk to anyone outside the family.
  • There is no advertising, we do not sell data and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • You can delete the account and all data at any time, in the app itself.

We do not ask for or process health data, location, contacts, address or payment data of children. We ask that you use only the first name or a nickname for your children and that you only upload photos you have the right to use.

3. Children

SAS Kids is intended to be managed by parents or other holders of parental responsibility who are of legal age. The account is always created by an adult, who enters the children's data and decides which devices may access it. Children do not create an account or provide an email address, and the app has no chats, public profiles or contact with people outside the family.

By creating the account, the account holder confirms that they are of legal age and have parental responsibility for the children they add (or the authorisation of whoever has it).

4. Legal basis

  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): processing account and family data to provide the service you requested.
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)): keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and keeping technical logs for a short time.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): device notifications, which are only sent if you turn them on and which you can turn off at any time.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): when the law requires us to keep or disclose data.

5. Who helps us provide the service

We use these processors, under contracts that require them to protect the data and to use it only to provide the service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited): accounts, database and notifications. The database is hosted on servers in Europe. Google may process some data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards provided for in the GDPR (EU-US adequacy decision and standard contractual clauses).
  • Website hosting provider: Proalojamento, with servers in the European Union.
  • When you turn on notifications, they are also delivered through your browser's or operating system's notification service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla).

We do not sell, rent or share data for advertising purposes.

6. How long we keep the data

  • For as long as the account exists. When you delete the account, the family's data is deleted immediately from the database. It may remain in our providers' backups until they are overwritten, normally for a maximum of 30 days.
  • Accounts that have not been used for more than 24 months may be deleted, after we send a notice to the account's email address.
  • Technical server logs: up to 90 days.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to rectify it, to erase it, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw your consent at any time. You can exercise most of these rights directly in the app:

  • Rectify: edit the profiles, tasks, rewards and history.
  • Erase: in ⚙️ Settings → Delete account and all data.
  • Notifications: tap the bell 🔔 to turn them off.

For any other requests, write to privacidade@saskids.net. We will reply within one month. If you believe your data is not being handled properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Portuguese Data Protection Authority, Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD) (www.cnpd.pt).

8. Storage on the device and cookies

The app stores on your device only what it needs to work: your logged-in session, the device's connection to the family and whether notifications are turned on. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. That is why we do not show a cookie notice: this storage is strictly necessary for the service you requested.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords and PINs are never stored as plain text and only the members of each family can read that family's data. The PINs protect each person's area within the family and do not replace the account password, which you should keep secure.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will notify you in the app or by email before the changes take effect.
